Windows tray app that shows the GameSir Cyclone 2 battery level on the controller LEDs and in the system tray. Written in TypeScript on top of `gamesir-hid` and packaged with Node.js as a single executable.

Download `cyclone2-battery.exe` and run it: no installer, nothing else to install. Windows 10/11 x64, wired or 2.4 GHz dongle.

Levels, menu and behavior: [examples/battery-led](https://github.com/pedrosegato/gamesir-reverse-engineering/tree/main/examples/battery-led).
