Windows tray app that shows the GameSir Cyclone 2 battery level on the Home LED and in the system tray.

Download `cyclone2-battery.exe` and run it: no installer, no runtime. Windows 10/11 x64, wired or 2.4 GHz dongle.

Levels, menu and behavior: [apps/battery-tray](https://github.com/pedrosegato/gamesir-reverse-engineering/tree/main/apps/battery-tray).
